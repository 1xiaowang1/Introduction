<strong>Abstract:</strong> We present a transformer-enhanced framework for dynamic structured-light 3D imaging that improves motion robustness and reconstruction accuracy for moving objects. The method combines correspondence-based motion tracking with pixel-wise phase-error compensation to recover high-quality 3D geometry under arbitrary motion.
